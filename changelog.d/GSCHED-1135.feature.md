A new Plan History tab, next to Visibility, shows every plan stored for a night: pick the night (or one of the recent nights with plans), see its plans on a timeline and in a list with their trigger, status (in effect, proposed, accepted, discarded, superseded or expired) and who decided, and open any of them in the same plot and tables as the Operation tab. Plans can be filtered by the scheduler mode of the server that made them.
Add the `generatedPlanNights`, `generatedPlans` and `generatedPlanContent` queries, and the `generated_plans.night_date` column (migration 014, which fills it in for plans already stored).
